What is Itzamna?

Itzamna is a small programming language written entirely in words. Numbers are Classic Maya number words, and operators, keywords and punctuation are words too. There are no digits, and the only brackets are the words pakal and kal. Line breaks have no meaning: every block is closed by the word kal, and the word mut runs everything collected so far.
The design goals are a small grammar, one job for every word, and semantics close to Python where possible.

What does the name Itzamna mean?

Itzamná was the creator god in the Maya religion. He was thought to have brought the art of writing and the calendar to humanity. The name might mean “lizard house” or “leguan house”.
